# Surface brief: pink.copperpeakair.com/chips/ (whole site, Paint Chip Deck world)

Scope: every page rendered in the Paint Chip Deck world. Mode: Persuade (home, pricing), Read (DIY, FYI).
Pinned: same as kitchen brief (pink-and-white default, duotone logo/favicon, customizer, readable everywhere, phone brightest, no gauges, no "for women" copy).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a paint-store chip deck: every palette is a named chip strip, and every page is a room painted in the chip you pulled. Refuses: a color picker hidden in a settings gear, and pastel-everything softness that hides the phone number.

OWN-WORLD: Chip cards: white card stock with a solid color block on top and a printed name and chip number below (e.g. "CP-101 Blush"); hole-punch dot on the deck. Archivo Black for headings, Work Sans for body, chip numbers in tabular Work Sans. Sections are rooms painted in the chosen chip's light wash, with chip-card service tiles.

STORY: The visitor sees a fanned deck of pink chips by the logo, pulls a different chip, and the room repaints; the phone number sits on the biggest chip card.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with email and call; hero left: logo in the chip duotone, headline, lead, call chip card; hero right: the fanned chip deck (five presets + a "Mix your own" blank chip) with a spacing control.

FORM: Paint Chip Deck, IMPECCABLE'S PICK, candidate 1 of my 7, seed key 1ec9c4d3. Signature: the deck fans open (rotation spread) and pulling a chip repaints the room; a floating deck button reopens it on every page.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
