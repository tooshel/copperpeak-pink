# Surface brief: pink.copperpeakair.com/kitchen/ (whole site, Enamel Kitchen world)

Scope: every page of the Copper Peak site rendered in the Enamel Kitchen world. Mode: Persuade (home, pricing), Read (DIY, FYI).
Audience/job/constraints: see PRODUCT.md. Pinned: pink-and-white default, pink-duotone logo and favicon, visitor customizer (palette presets + custom colors + spacing), readable text in every palette, phone always the brightest object, no gauges, no "for women" copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a bright white-cabinet kitchen dressed in glossy pink enamelware; the customizer is the appliance-brochure color picker, "Choose your finish." Refuses: the stock contractor template recolored pink, and any girly cliché (hearts, script-everything, glitter).

OWN-WORLD: White lacquer cabinet panels (soft inset frame lines) as the ground; glossy enamel in the accent color for buttons, badges, and section bands, lit by a top highlight; thin chrome trim rules. Display face Shrikhand (retro appliance badge) used sparingly for headings; Figtree for everything else. Finish swatches are round enamel dots with names underneath.

STORY: A hot visitor sees the phone number on an enamel badge immediately, notices the finish swatches, taps one, and the whole kitchen repaints; then they read prices, guides, or call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Chrome-trim top bar with email and enamel call badge; cabinet-panel hero, logo duotoned in the finish (left), headline and lead (right), a large enamel call button, and the "Choose your finish" swatch row with a Custom chip and spacing toggle beneath.

FORM: Enamel Kitchen, THE ROLL, candidate 3 of my 7, seed key 1ec9c4d3. Signature: tap a finish swatch and the site repaints in one smooth wash; buttons depress like appliance buttons.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
