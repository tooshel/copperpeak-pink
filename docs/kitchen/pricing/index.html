Up-front pricing. Yes, really.

Every situation is different, of course. But we're up front about pricing. Why? Because almost no one else is, and we want to change that.

Why nobody tells you the price on the phone

Call around and you'll hear the same thing: "We'll have to send someone out." Here's the secret: every company already has a standard price book. They know what a capacitor swap or a new 5-ton system costs before they ever see your house.

The reason they won't say it is simple. Once a friendly tech is standing in your kitchen on a 112° afternoon, you're a lot more likely to say yes to a higher price.

Our techs are charming too (and, we're told, handsome), and they want to keep you cool all summer long. But we think we can win your business by being straight with you first.

And if you take these numbers to someone else and they come in cheaper because you called them first, or showed them this page? Well, you can thank us for that discount.

Why the big companies cost more

Big HVAC companies carry overhead ten layers deep: call centers, marketing budgets, sales managers, fleets of branded trucks, and owners who aren't on the jobs. All of it gets paid for out of your repair bill.

So ask the two questions that matter. Are they paying their techs more? Not in our experience. Are their techs better? Also no. The person who shows up does the same work. You're just paying for everything behind them.

We're confident we're cheaper than the big companies, and competitive with (and, we think, better than) the other small shops. You might not go with us, and that's fine. But don't go with the big guys.
